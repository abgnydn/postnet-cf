Phase 2 · delta-only broadcast

Postnet × Cloudflare — tournament

Same 2D classifier task as the federated-Adam page, but a different aggregation protocol: each worker locally tries K random flips, submits its best (indices, values, Δloss), and the coordinator picks the single best across all workers in the round. Phase 2: workers bootstrap θ once via /api/tournament/snapshot and maintain it locally. The coord ships only the accepted flip (~36 B) per round — both uplink and downlink are now independent of model size.
